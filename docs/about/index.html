About

Hi! I'm the creator behind Yzaverse Puzzle Books.

I started making puzzle books because I wanted something simple, screen-free, and fun for all ages. Puzzles have always been a go-to for me when I need to slow down and focus, and I wanted to share that with others.

What I Make

  • Sudoku for kids with easy 4x4 grids and friendly mascots, designed for ages 6 to 8
  • Sudoku for adults with classic 9x9 puzzles for quiet relaxation
  • Word search with NYC-themed puzzles in large, easy-to-read print

Every book includes solutions and is available in paperback on Amazon.

Why Puzzles?

Puzzles are portable, timeless, and don't need a charger. They help kids build confidence and give adults a break from screens. I care about the details: the themes, the print size, and the paper quality all matter.
