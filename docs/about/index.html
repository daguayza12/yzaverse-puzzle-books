About

Hi! I'm the creator behind Yzaverse Puzzle Books.

I make puzzle books because I believe everyone deserves a quiet moment to focus, unwind, and feel accomplished — no screens required. Whether it's a child solving their very first sudoku or an adult unwinding after a long day, puzzles have a way of bringing calm and joy.

What I Make

  • Sudoku for kids — easy 4x4 grids with friendly mascots, designed for ages 6–8
  • Sudoku for adults — classic 9x9 puzzles for calm, focused relaxation
  • Word search — NYC-themed puzzles in large, easy-to-read print

Every book includes solutions, is thoughtfully designed, and is available in paperback on Amazon.

Why Puzzles?

Puzzles are screen-free, portable, and timeless. They build confidence in kids, sharpen focus in adults, and make thoughtful gifts for anyone who enjoys a good challenge. I put care into every page — from the themes to the print size to the paper quality.
